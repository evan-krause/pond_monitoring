| Parameter | Recommended graphic | Why |
|----|----|----|
| temp_c | Heatmap (temp_c_lp2, temp_c_lp3) | The thermocline depth/shape is the interesting year-over-year signal; a boxplot collapses depth and loses that. |
| do | Heatmap (do_lp2, do_lp3) | Bottom-water oxygen depletion (hypolimnetic anoxia) is depth-resolved and often the key management message — a boxplot can't show where low DO occurs. |
| chla | Heatmap (chla_lp2, chla_lp3) | Bloom timing and depth (surface vs. subsurface) differed between years in what we saw earlier; that pattern disappears in a boxplot. |
| spc | Boxplot (spc) | Confirmed via spc_lp2/spc_lp3 above — conductivity is fairly uniform with depth at both sites; the real story is a level shift between 2025 and 2026 (LP2: \~140 → \~180-200 µS/cm; LP3: \~150-170 → \~170-190 µS/cm), which a single boxplot communicates more directly than two dense heatmap panels with independent color scales. |
